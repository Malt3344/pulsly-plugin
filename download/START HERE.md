# Start here

This folder turns your Claude into a video studio for your business. Setting it up takes
about 10 minutes, once. You only click; Claude does the technical part.

## What you need

- **Claude Pro or Max** (claude.ai/upgrade).
- **The Claude app** for Mac or Windows: download it from **claude.ai/download** and log in.
- **An ElevenLabs account** for a natural voice (optional, recommended). You connect it in Pulsly
  under **Settings → Voice**; it takes two minutes.

## Set it up

1. **Unzip this folder** if you haven't already (Mac: double-click the zip; Windows:
   right-click it → **Extract All**). Move it somewhere you'll keep it, like **Documents**.
2. **Open the Claude app** and click **Code** at the top.
3. **Choose this folder:** click the folder picker and select the **Pulsly** folder (or drag the
   folder into the window).
4. **Send this message:** `Set up Pulsly for me`
5. **Follow Claude.** It takes you through 5 short steps, one at a time. When it asks for
   permission to run something, click **Allow**. When your browser opens Pulsly, click
   **Allow** there too.

## Make your first video

1. Go to **app.pulsly.co/studio**.
2. Pick a style (3D, paper, motion graphics, blocky 3D story, story + gameplay, text chat or
   quiz), write one line about what the video should be about, and click **Make the
   video**.
3. Claude starts within seconds and needs 20–40 minutes. Keep your computer on.
4. When it says **Ready to review**, click **Watch the video**. Nothing is posted until you
   approve it.

Want new videos every week by themselves? Turn on **Content engine** in Pulsly.

## If something is stuck

Open this folder in the Claude app again and tell Claude what you see, for example
"My video has been waiting for an hour". It checks and fixes it with you.
